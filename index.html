Who we are

A small team that actually turns up

T&N Plumbing and Projects (Pty) Ltd has been providing first-class services at reasonable rates since 2017. What began as one plumber with a bakkie and a phone is now a tight team working across Johannesburg — still run hands-on by Themba, who is on site or on the phone for every job.

  • Based in Malvern, Johannesburg
  • Homes, offices and rentals
  • Repairs and full renovations
  • Open seven days a week

You call, we look at the job properly, and you get the price before anyone picks up a spanner. No surprise line items at the end. When the work is done we show you what was replaced and why, then we clean up behind ourselves — that part is not optional.

  • Quote agreed before we start
  • We explain the fix in plain English
  • Old parts shown to you
  • Site left clean each day
